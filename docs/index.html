A robot that notices its body changed, and proves the fix.

AIRO runs on a $50 car with no wheel encoders and one overhead phone camera. When a load, a tow or a worn motor makes its body model wrong, it notices, designs its own experiments with Muse Wonder, refits a physics model, and only accepts it once a prediction lands. Every body it learns goes into a memory the whole fleet shares.

01 The problem

Bodies change. Models don't.

Every robot drives by a model of its own body, tuned once by an engineer. Then it carries a heavy tote, tows a cart, its tyres wear or its battery sags. The model is now wrong, and the robot stops short or drifts without knowing it.

Today the fix is a service call. Over a robot's life, setup, tuning and upkeep cost more than the robot itself.

04 The loop

Notice, test, refit, verify, remember.

Meta's models plan the experiments, look at the car, read the floor and take commands. They never steer: the 20 Hz control loop runs on the fitted physics, and every number a model quotes comes from a tool.

The full technical write-up →

  1. 01 · notice

    Surprise against the cloud

    Every half second the car's motion is scored against its predicted futures. The median of three windows over threshold, or a slow drift summed by a CUSUM, means the body changed.

  2. 02 · experiment

    Muse Wonder designs the probe

    A step, a pivot or an arc, each with a falsifiable hypothesis. The physics model predicts the outcome before the wheels turn.

  3. 03 · refit

    Seven-parameter body

    Per-side gain, dead zone, delay, wheel lag, turn efficiency and drift, fitted to what the camera saw, with a Laplace posterior that becomes the new cloud.

  4. 04 · verify

    Only a landed prediction counts

    A relearn is accepted once a tight prediction lands within 2σ on distance, sideways drift and turn. A refit with a motor's sign flipped finds a reversed wire.

  5. 05 · remember

    Fleet memory

    Each body is stored for the robot model and shared. Another robot that meets a known body recalls it with one confirming probe in about 2.1 s.

  6. 06 · listen

    Plain words in

    Muse Spark turns "airo 2 bring a heavy tote to packing" into tool calls, Muse Voice Transcribe hears it, SAM 3.1 reads the floor, Muse Image draws each body's self-portrait.

06 Try it yourself

The factory twin in 60 seconds. No API key.

Needs uv, which fetches Python 3.13. Without a key, a scripted scientist runs a fixed probe schedule and the twin segments its own frames, so everything runs offline.

# clone, install, run
git clone https://github.com/airo7/airo-meta
cd airo-meta
uv sync
uv run scripts/demo.py --site factory
# open http://127.0.0.1:8000

uv run scripts/fetch_models.py adds the UR5e arm, the Robotiq gripper and the G1 workers from MuJoCo Menagerie. With a MODEL_API_KEY in .env, uv run --env-file .env scripts/demo.py --site factory uses the Meta models live.

Then type into the command bar

airo 2 bring a heavy tote to packingan arm loads it, the car checks its body, delivers
select airo 3, learnpick a robot and make it learn its body
weight, tow, flipchange its body mid-run; watch it notice
remove weightback to a body it already knows: recall
send a worker across, drop a cartonthings the fleet plans around
busy, speed 0.5, pausehow lively the floor is, and the clock

Drag to orbit the 3D view, scroll to zoom, click a part to name it.

07 Meta models

Five models, one job each, none in the control loop.

ModelRole in AIRO
muse-wonder-1.0The scientist. Picks each probe with a falsifiable hypothesis, decides whether to recall a remembered body or relearn, concludes and names the body. Its tools return every number; it never steers. A 12 s budget per relearn, then a scripted policy finishes.
muse-spark-1.3Turns typed or spoken commands into tool calls (go to, turn, push, patrol, learn, say, deliver) from the robot's pose and floor. Looks at the car when its body changes and says what is on it. "Stop" never waits for it.
sam-3.1Segments the floor: totes, stations and obstacles become polygons and an occupancy grid for the planner.
muse-voice-transcribe-1.0Realtime speech to commands.
muse-image-1.0A self-portrait of each confirmed body for its memory card.

Every call appears live in the dashboard's model feed with its latency, input and output.
